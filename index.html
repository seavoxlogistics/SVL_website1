Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Is SeaVox Logistics specialized in importing goods from China to Pakistan? +

Yes! Importing goods into Pakistan from China is our core specialty. We manage the entire trade corridor including Guangzhou and Yiwu sourcing, RMB supplier payment assistance, express air cargo (3-5 days), ocean freight (FCL & LCL to Karachi Port), WeBOC customs clearance, and Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) door-to-door cargo delivery to all cities across Pakistan.

How does Door-to-Door (DDP) import shipping to Pakistan work? +

Under our DDP service, SeaVox Logistics handles everything for Pakistani importers: factory pickup in China, export documentation, ocean/air shipping, Pakistan customs duties, taxes, WeBOC electronic clearance, and final delivery directly to your store or warehouse in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Sialkot, or Multan.

Do you handle WeBOC customs clearance in Karachi and Islamabad? +

Yes. Our in-house licensed customs clearing agents manage WeBOC electronic Goods Declaration (GD) filing, duty assessment, HS code classification, and port clearance at Karachi Port (KICT, PICT, SAPT), Port Qasim, Lahore Dry Port, and Islamabad International Airport.

How long does shipping from China to Pakistan take? +

Transit duration depends on the transport mode: Express Air Cargo takes 3 to 5 days, while Ocean Cargo to Karachi Port takes 18 to 25 days depending on the departure port (Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Ningbo) and customs processing.

Do you assist with China factory sourcing & RMB payments? +

Yes! Our bilingual sourcing desks in Guangzhou and Yiwu provide direct factory verification, supplier audits, price negotiation, RMB supplier payment assistance, and pre-shipment quality control inspections across China.
